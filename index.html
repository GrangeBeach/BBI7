The artists

Two composers creating original music without a genre boundary.

BBI7 is the artist identity of two co-composers Americo Lopresti and Vincent Scaffidi whose work brings orchestral writing together with contemporary textures, rhythm and songcraft.

The result is original orchestral fusion: cinematic without being confined to film, melodic without being predictable, and deliberately independent of a single genre. Music for people who don't just fit into one box.

Every BBI7 composition is original, with the associated rights retained by the artists.
